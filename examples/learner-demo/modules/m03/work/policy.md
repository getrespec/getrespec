# Inkwell Books — Returns policy

§1 Printed books can be returned within 14 days of delivery, unread and in their original condition, with the receipt. Opened box sets can be returned if every volume is included.
§2 Books that arrive damaged or misprinted are refunded in full, including shipping.
§3 E-books, audiobooks, and gift cards can't be returned.
§4 Gifts returned without a receipt are refunded as store credit.
§5 Items bought as part of a bundle are returned as a bundle only.
