# Assignment — A grounded refund assistant

## Scenario
Support wants a bot that answers refund questions only from the policy in `work/policy.md`, and says "I don't know" otherwise.

## Deliverables
- `work/prompt.md` — the system prompt and any examples
- `work/cases.md` — 10 test questions with expected answers, and what the bot actually said

## Rubric
| Item | Weight |
|---|---|
| Answers only from the policy | 35 |
| Says "I don't know" when it should | 25 |
| Consistent format | 20 |
| Reasoning and understanding | 20 |
